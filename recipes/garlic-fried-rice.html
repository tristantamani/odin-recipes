Garlic Fried Rice Recipe

Description

This recipe is a stable in Filipino breakfast, often called as "Sinangag". This dish is made of leftover rice, garlic, salt, and ground pepper. Other variations use scrambled eggs and spring onions but this version is a simple and easy recipe only.

Ingredients

  • Leftover white rice, preferably medium grain
  • 1 head garlic, minced or sliced based on preference
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and ground pepper to taste
