Garlic Fried Rice Recipe

Description

This recipe is a stable in Filipino breakfast, often called as "Sinangag". This dish is made of leftover rice, garlic, salt, and ground pepper. Other variations use scrambled eggs and spring onions but this version is a simple and easy recipe only.

Ingredients

  • Leftover white rice, preferably medium grain
  • 1 head garlic, minced or sliced based on preference
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and ground pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Place the day old medium grain rice in a separate bowl.
  2. Heat a wide cooking pan over medium heat and pour in the cooking oil.
  3. Once the oil is hot, add the minced garlic and saute until light brown.
  4. Add half of the leftover rice to the pan and stir fry for about a minute.
  5. Spread the rice in the pan and let it sit for 30-45 seconds.
  6. Add salt and pepper to taste, then mix thoroughly
  7. After mixing, add the second half of the leftover rice and mix again.
  8. After about 3-5 minutes, taste it again and add salt and pepper as needed.
  9. Turn off the heat and transfer it on a serving plate, serve and enjoy!
