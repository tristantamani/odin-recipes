Pastil Recipe

Description

This recipe is an homemade pastil recipe made by my girlfriend. Pastil is a dish made with shredded chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, and pepper mixed then placed on top of white rice and served with a banana leaf.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 or 1 whole chicken breast
  • Cooking oil
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 head garlic
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Ground pepper
  • Sugar
  • Turmeric powder (optional)

Steps

  1. Pour water in a deep cooking pot, add the chicken breast and let it boil.
  2. While waiting, mince onion and garlic. You may also prepare the other ingredients.
  3. After boiling the chicken breast, slice it into small strips.
  4. Prepare a clean cooking pan, add cooking oil and let it heat.
  5. Saute onion and garlic in the pan.
  6. Add the chicken breast strips and saute until the fat renders.
  7. Add soy sauce and vinegar in 2:1 ratio to taste.
  8. Add ground pepper and sugar to blend the flavors and mix thoroughly.
  9. Turn off the heat and transfer it on a serving plate, serve and enjoy!
