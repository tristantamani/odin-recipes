Carbonara Recipe

Description

This recipe is a Filipino-style creamy carbonara made by my girlfriend. This version uses spaghetti pasta, tuna, shredded chicken, and mushrooms. It is also halal and does not contain any bacon or any pork meat.

Ingredients

  • 300-400 g spaghetti pasta
  • 1 head garlic
  • 1 can of Century Tuna
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 kg shredded chicken breast
  • 1/2 cup of button mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 all purpose cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated parmesan cheese
  • Spinach leaves (optional)

Steps

  1. Pour water in a deep cooking pot, add salt, and let it boil.
  2. Put the spaghetti into the pot then cook the spaghetti according to the time indicated in the package instructions.
  3. Remove the spaghetti from the cooking pot, drain the water, then set aside.
  4. Prepare the sauce by heating a clean cooking pan.
  5. Melt the butter in a pan then saute garlic until light brown.
  6. Add sliced mushrooms, mix and cook for 30 seconds.
  7. Pour the all-purpose cream into the pot, then stir and cook for 3 to 4 minutes.
  8. Add the Parmesan cheese and stir until it becomes smooth.
  9. Add in the cooked pasta earlier in the cooking pan and mix gently and thoroughly.
  10. Turn off the heat and transfer it on a large bowl, serve and enjoy!
